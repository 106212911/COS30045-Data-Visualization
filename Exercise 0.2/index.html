Energy Consumption on appliances in Australia.

Household that own multiple electrical appliances use more than half of a home's total electricity. Houses that mainly have multiple air-conditioners are considered the top usage of power especially brands that lack in power efficiency.

While smaller electrical appliances consume less power, as an overall, it does consume quite an amount of power which increases household's electricity bills up to an unexpected amount.

To help consumers make informed choices, Australia uses a mandatory Energy Rating Label on major appliances. The label features a star rating system, ranging from 1 to 6 (or more) stars, where a higher star rating indicates greater energy efficiency. Choosing appliances with higher ratings can reduce running costs and lower environmental impact over the appliance's lifetime.
